Honor Emoji Set Now Available

Aug 31, 2026 · 6 min read

Honor’s latest MagicOS update includes a new in-house emoji set. Faces, gestures, and everyday objects get a cleaner, slightly more 3D look than the previous system font on Honor phones.

What changed

The new designs keep Unicode meanings but redraw outlines, color, and lighting. Smileys are rounder. Flags and symbols stay flat so they remain readable in lists. Skin-tone sequences and family ZWJ sequences are included so existing messages do not fall back to boxes.

Where you will see them

  • Honor keyboards and system UI after the MagicOS update.
  • Apps that use the device emoji font instead of their own pack.
  • Screenshots and chats sent from updated Honor phones.

Other phones will still show Apple, Google, Samsung, or Microsoft artwork for the same characters. Copy and paste still transfers the Unicode, not Honor’s pictures.

Why vendor sets matter

Emoji are characters. The pictures are fonts. A new Honor set does not add new characters by itself, but it does change how those characters look for millions of devices. Comparing vendor art is one of the main reasons people keep emoji reference sites open.
