What's New On World Emoji Day 2026

Jul 17, 2026 · 5 min read

World Emoji Day lands on July 17 each year, taken from the calendar emoji. This year is the twelfth annual celebration of new designs, vendor updates, and a few awards that the internet actually argues about.

The usual mix

  • Draft lists for the next Unicode emoji cycle.
  • Sample art from phone makers.
  • Polls for most anticipated, most used, and lifetime awards.

The day is unofficial. Unicode does not ship a standard because of it. Brands, keyboards, and fans use the date to talk about what should be added next and which designs people actually send.

What stood out this year

Food and faces still dominate requests. Travel landmarks and a few office objects showed up in shortlists. The fire emoji took the lifetime award, which surprised nobody who has used a group chat since 2015.

If you want a practical way to mark the day, open the New and Travel tabs, copy a few characters you never use, and see which ones your friends can already render.
