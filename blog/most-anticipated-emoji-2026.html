The Most Anticipated Emoji of 2026 is...

Jul 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Ahead of Emoji 18.0, we asked which draft candidates people wanted first. Food and faces won, again. The pickle and the cracking face led most replies, with the lighthouse as a surprise third.

Why those three

  • Pickle fills a gap in the food drawer that people mention every year.
  • Cracking Face gives a reaction that is not already covered by grin, sweat, or exploding head.
  • Lighthouse is specific enough to be useful in travel and “safe harbor” jokes.

Anticipation vs. actual use

Most anticipated does not mean most sent. Heart, fire, and the basic smileys still dominate real traffic. New characters get a spike in the first weeks after a keyboard update, then settle into niche use.

When 18.0 fonts ship on your phone, the New tab is the fastest way to copy them. Until then, drafts are just pictures. The live characters appear only after Unicode and vendors both ship.
