Classic Noto Color Emoji Lives On As Google Debuts Emoji 18.0 Support

Sep 16, 2026 · 3 min read

On the same day that Unicode 18.0 and Emoji 18.0 were formally approved, Google released classic Noto Color Emoji designs for the newest set. That means the familiar 2D Noto look is not going away, even as 3D work continues on Pixel devices.

What arrived

The update covers the newly approved emoji, including faces, food, objects, and a handful of long-requested symbols. Designers kept the rounded outlines, saturated fills, and simple shading that made Noto easy to read at small sizes.

Google shipped the classic set first so apps, websites, and Android versions that still use 2D emoji can pick up Emoji 18.0 without waiting for the 3D overhaul.

Why the classic set still matters

  • Most Android phones still render 2D Noto, not the Pixel 11 3D pack.
  • Web fonts and chat apps often depend on the classic assets.
  • A shared 2D set keeps emoji readable in lists, buttons, and search results.

If you copy emoji from this site, the characters themselves are Unicode. The pictures you see on a phone or laptop come from the local font. Classic Noto remains one of the most widely used of those fonts.
