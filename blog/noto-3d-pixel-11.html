Google Debuts Noto 3D Emoji Overhaul on Pixel 11

Aug 21, 2026 · 8 min read

The Pixel 11 range ships a full 3D redraw of Noto Color Emoji. Google previewed the direction at Android I/O. The production set now covers smileys, people, food, travel, and objects with thicker volume and softer lighting.

How it looks

Faces have deeper shading and more distinct mouths. Food looks rounder. Vehicles and landmarks pick up small highlights so they read as objects, not flat stickers. The goal is a set that still works at 16px in a chat bubble.

Pixel first, not Android everywhere

Classic 2D Noto remains the default on many Android skins. Pixel 11 is the first device family to use the 3D pack system-wide. Other phones may follow in later Android releases, or keep their own vendor fonts.

  • Gboard on Pixel 11 shows the 3D art in the picker.
  • Messages and system UI use the same font.
  • Websites still show whatever font the browser has.

This site uses Fluent 3D-style images for browsing, then copies the Unicode character. That matches how most people share emoji: pictures locally, characters on the wire.
