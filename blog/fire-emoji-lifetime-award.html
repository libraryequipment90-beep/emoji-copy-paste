Lifetime Achievement Award: The Fire Emoji

Jul 17, 2026 · 2 min read

The fire emoji is this year’s lifetime achievement pick. Few characters have stayed useful for so long without needing a redesign of their meaning.

Why it lasts

🔥 started as a literal flame. It became praise, hype, attraction, and a one-tap reply that works in almost any thread. That range is the point of a good emoji: one picture, many jobs.

  • Compliment a photo or a take.
  • Mark something as popular or “going viral.”
  • Stand in for heat, cooking, or actual fire when the context is clear.

Vendor art still disagrees on how orange the flame should be, but everyone recognizes it. That consistency is why it belongs in a lifetime list more than last year’s joke character.

Find it under Smileys-adjacent reaction use, or just search “fire” on the home page and copy it.
