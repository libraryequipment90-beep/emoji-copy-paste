Unicode Emoji Update

What's New In Unicode 18.0

Sep 16, 2026 · 6 min read

Unicode 18.0 locks in the next emoji list. Vendors can now ship final designs instead of draft artwork. The set is smaller than some past years, but the additions are specific and easy to use in everyday chat.

Highlights

  • Cracking Face — a face splitting into a grin or grimace.
  • Pickle — a long-requested food emoji.
  • Meteor — for space news, jokes, and sudden events.
  • Lighthouse — a clear landmark for travel and guidance.
  • Eraser — useful in school, design, and “undo that” jokes.

How rollout works

Unicode publishes the characters. Phone makers, chat apps, and font foundries then draw them. Google, Apple, Microsoft, Samsung, and others usually land within months of each other, but the pictures will not match exactly.

Until your device has the new font, a missing emoji may show as a box or a last-resort outline. Copying the character still works. The receiver only sees the picture if their font includes it too.

What to watch next

Expect vendor sample sheets, keyboard updates, and a few design debates as each company interprets the same code points. The New tab on this site is the place to browse additions as they land in common fonts.
