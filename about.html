About Get Emoji

Last updated October 17, 2026

Get Emoji is a free, static website for copying emoji as plain Unicode text. Click any emoji to copy it, then paste it into chat, email, documents, or social posts with Ctrl+V or Command+V. You do not need an account or an app.

How to copy an emoji

  • Browse a category tab or search by name, keyword, or category.
  • Click or tap the emoji once. A toast confirms when the copy succeeds.
  • Right-click, long-press, or press I on a focused emoji to open its details, then copy or save it as a favorite.
  • Favorites and recently used emoji stay in this browser only.

What you will find

Smileys, people, animals, food, travel, objects, symbols, flags, and newer Unicode additions. Tools such as text-to-emoji convert, mix, mood search, download, recolor, combos, games, avatars, and a small static JSON catalog are included as extras. The mix, recolor, and download tools draw what this browser can render; they do not create new official Unicode characters.

Data sources

Emoji are Unicode characters. Names and search keywords come from public emoji catalogs. 3D pictures come from Fluent Emoji (via jsDelivr). Flag pictures come from Twemoji. Cartoon avatars come from DiceBear. This site is not affiliated with Unicode, Google, Apple, Microsoft, Twitter/X, or DiceBear.
